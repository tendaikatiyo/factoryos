# Operations Audit Plan and Schedule

Prepared by: Tendai A.F. Katiyo

Prepared for: Rawplast Investments Pvt. Ltd.

Date: 18 Aug 2026

### Executive summary

-   Purpose: Identify operational gaps and quick wins across four sites (Msasa HQ, Southerton, Harare CBD, Bulawayo), focusing on production performance, safety, quality and order fulfilment.
-   Ask: access to records, short staff interviews, and permission to photograph shop floors for evidence.
-   Approach: staged multi-week assessment focused on production first, followed by targeted deep-dive costing.

### Scope

-   Sites: Msasa HQ (production + sales), Southerton (production), Harare CBD (sales), Bulawayo (sales — different market).
-   Departments: Production, Planning, Despatch/Receiving, Sales/order capture, and related admin.

### Schedule

*Start date: 24 August 2026*

| Phase | Activities |
|--------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| Week 1–2 (Msasa HQ) | Deep production focus; shift observations (day/night); maintenance records; production planning; despatch. |
| Week 3 (Southerton) | Production evaluation; capacity & SOP alignment with HQ; minimal sales shop pre-opening checks. |
| Week 4 (Bulawayo + Harare CBD) | Bulawayo + Harare CBD site assessments. Evaluation of walk-in shops operations |
| Week 5 | Consolidation; draft consolidated report; prioritise findings. |
| Week 6 | Deep-dive costing and implementation-ready plans for priority items. |

### Deliverables & timelines

| Deliverable | Timeline / Notes |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Per-site short report | Within 48 hours after conclusion of site visit — 2–4 pages with top 5 findings, recommended owners, estimated effort. |
| Consolidated report | Within 5 working days after final site visit — prioritised action plan with estimated costs/time for implementation. |

### Resource & access requests

-   Permission to photograph and interview staff for audit evidence.

### Success criteria

-   Top 5 actionable improvements per site with owners and target dates.
-   Prioritised implementation plan with rough cost estimates for approval.
