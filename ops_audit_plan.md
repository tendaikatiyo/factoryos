Operations Audit Plan and Schedule

Purpose
- Conduct onsite assessments across four locations (Msasa HQ, Southerton, Bulawayo, Harare CBD) focusing primarily on production facilities and processes, and produce actionable reports for the director.

Principles
- Spend majority of time in production (≈60% of total assessment time).
- Use standard checklist and evidence capture (photos, timestamps, staff interviews).
- Prioritise quick wins and safety/compliance issues.

Shift operations
- The factory operates two primary shifts: 07:00–19:00 and 19:00–07:00. Printers operate across three shifts (times TBC).
- Assessment implication: observe at least one daytime and one nighttime production shift where possible, and prioritise printers for multi-shift observation because their performance and issues can vary by shift.
- Schedule night-shift visits in coordination with site security and site managers; ensure safety measures (escorts, lighting, PPE) and record which shift is observed in the evidence log.
- Focus during shift observations: handover quality, operator numbers, supervision levels, machine warm-up and changeover procedures, maintenance tasks at shift change, fatigue/overtime indicators, and any quality variance between shifts.

Suggested 5–6 week schedule (recommended)

Week 1–2 — Msasa HQ (deep production focus; start here)
- Day 1–2: Production — plant tour, safety, flow mapping, shadow shifts
- Day 3: Production — sample runs, quality checks, maintenance records review
- Day 4: Production planning & materials (MRP/BOM review, scheduling accuracy)
- Day 5: Despatch / Purchasing / Receiving (warehouse layout, inbound checks, stock accuracy)
- Day 6: Sales shop & order capture (retail operations, custom order process)
- Day 7: Management interviews, records reconciliation, compile interim notes
- Day 8–10: Follow-ups, repeat observations, targeted tests

Week 3 — Southerton (production facility focus; sales shop lower priority)
- Context: Southerton already operates a production facility (~70% smaller than HQ). The sales shop is not yet open and pre-opening plans are unclear, so prioritise production operations and process alignment with HQ.
- Day 1: Site readiness walk (utilities, layout, safety, permits)
- Day 2–3: Production operations review (machines, capacity, process flow, SOPs, training needs)
- Day 4: Production outputs and quality controls; compare to HQ standards
- Day 5: Purchasing/receiving & despatch processes for the plant; supply chain readiness
- Day 6: Minimal sales shop pre-opening checks only if required; staff handover planning
- Day 7: Buffer / follow-ups

Week 4 — Bulawayo & Harare CBD (sales shops)
- Bulawayo (sales shop + local supplier checks)
- Day 1: Sales shop operations, order capture, customer experience
- Day 2: Custom order processing and communications
- Day 3: Local supplier links, logistics and stock replenishment
- Day 4: Follow-ups and compile interim notes
- Harare CBD (sales shop only)
- 1 working day: Sales shop operations review, order capture, custom order intake, local customer interactions, and quick stock checks
- Note: Harare CBD is sales-only; no production assessment required unless director requests it

Week 5 — Consolidation & reporting
- Day 1–3: Draft consolidated report (executive summary, top risks, quick wins)
- Day 4: Prioritise findings and plan deep-dive items needing costing/validation
- Day 5: Finalise presentation for director

Week 6 (optional/additional) — Deep-dive follow-ups & costing
- 5–10 working days (site-specific) for detailed root-cause analysis, vendor quotes, piloting, and implementation-ready cost estimates


Time allocation guidance
- Production (on-site plant): ~60% of total time
- Purchasing/Despatch/Receiving: ~15% of time
- Sales/Order capture/Marketing: ~15% of time
- HR/Admin/process review & reporting: ~10% of time

Deliverables
- Per-site short report (2–4 pages) with top 5 findings and recommended actions
- Consolidated report with prioritised action plan and estimated costs/timelines
- Presentation slide deck for director summarising findings and asks

Notes
- For Southerton, prioritise the production facility (running at ~70% smaller capacity than HQ); keep sales shop pre-opening checks minimal unless director requests more focus
- Use `assessment_checklist.md` (same folder) to record observations
- Photograph evidence and attach to site reports
