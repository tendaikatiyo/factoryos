Operations Audit SOP — Field Guide for Tendai A.F. Katiyo

Purpose
- Standardise how onsite audits are conducted, evidence captured, and findings reported so recommendations are reliable and repeatable.

Owner
- Lead assessor: Tendai A.F. Katiyo (you)

Pre-visit (48–72 hours before site visit)
- Request from site contact:
  - Latest production plans and WO lists for the last 4 weeks
  - Maintenance logs and downtime records
  - Inventory records for critical SKUs and raw materials
  - Shift rosters (including printers' 3-shift schedule)
  - SOPs and work instructions for critical processes
  - Safety incident logs and training records
- Confirm site contact, security, and photo permissions.
- Prepare tools: `assessment_checklist.md`, camera/phone, notebook, voice recorder (optional), and CSV tracker.
- Create a site folder named `site-SITENAME-YYYY-MM-DD` on your working drive for photos and logs.

On-site procedure (step-by-step)
1. Management brief (10–20 minutes)
  - Introduce yourself and the audit purpose.
  - Confirm scope for the day and any sensitive areas.
  - Request key staff availability for interviews.
2. Safety check and PPE
  - Ensure you have required PPE and sign in with security.
3. Walk the floor (tour)
  - Observe flow, bottlenecks, storage, and housekeeping.
  - Take overview photos (label them using the naming convention).
4. Shift observations
  - Observe at least one full shift where possible; for compressed visits, observe handovers and peak operations.
  - For printers, confirm the 3-shift schedule and observe at least one changeover.
  - Fill the Shift observation log in `assessment_checklist.md`.
5. Targeted checks per department
  - Production: machinery condition, SOPs, WIP, QC checks, maintenance practice.
  - Planning: master schedule vs actual performance, BOM accuracy, changeover planning.
  - Despatch/Receiving: inbound checks, inventory accuracy, picking/packing standards.
  - Sales/shop: order capture for custom orders, POS accuracy, customer lead-time communication.
6. Staff interviews (5–15 minutes each)
  - Interview supervisors and 1–2 frontline operators per critical area.
  - Confirm actual vs documented practice and note any workarounds.
7. Evidence capture and logging
  - Use photo naming: SITE_YYYYMMDD_AREA_SHORT.jpg (e.g., MSASA_20260824_PRINTER1_WEAR.jpg).
  - Log findings immediately into `assessment_checklist.md` or a CSV row with ID, severity, owner, and follow-up date.
8. Immediate actions
  - If safety-critical issues found, stop and escalate to site manager and director (phone + photo). Record time and action taken.
9. End-of-day wrap
  - Short debrief with site manager (10 minutes): summarise immediate findings and any required temporary fixes.
  - Confirm point-of-contact for follow-up questions.

Finding severity and categorisation
- Severity 5 (Critical): Immediate risk to life/major regulatory breach — escalate and require immediate action.
- Severity 4 (High): Major production or quality risk requiring action within 48 hours.
- Severity 3 (Medium): Important efficiency/quality issues to be addressed within 2–4 weeks.
- Severity 2 (Low): Minor issues and housekeeping improvements.
- Severity 1 (Info): Observations and suggestions.

Reporting
- Per-site short report (template below): Submit within 48 hours. Include executive summary, top 5 findings (with severity), quick wins (0–2 weeks), medium-term actions (2–8 weeks), longer-term recommendations, photo appendix, and owner assignments.
- Consolidated report: submit within 5 working days after final site visit. Include prioritised action plan and rough cost/time estimates for items requiring funding.

Follow-up & deep-dive process
- For each finding requiring costing or pilot, create a follow-up ticket with scope, required resources, and an owner.
- Schedule deep-dive within 2–4 weeks for medium/major items, or as director approves.

Data handling & naming conventions
- Photos: SITE_YYYYMMDD_LOCATION_SHORT.jpg
- Checklist rows: CSV with columns ID, Site, Department, Machine, WO, Summary, Severity, Evidence, Owner, ETA, Status
- Reports and slides: store in `reports/YYYY-MM` folder and attach raw logs.

Confidentiality & staff interviews
- Explain to staff that the audit is for operational improvement, not disciplinary action.
- Do not record personal data beyond role and name; respect privacy when taking photos near staff.

Sign-off
- Site manager to sign the per-site short report acknowledgement (email or signature) within 48 hours.
- Director to approve consolidated prioritised plan before implementation of funded items.
