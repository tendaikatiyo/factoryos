Department-specific checkpoints

Production (both plants)
- Floor layout: logical flow, cross-traffic, bottlenecks
- Workstation ergonomics and tooling
- Machine condition, maintenance records, downtime log
- Standard Work: documented SOPs, visible instructions
- Quality control: sampling frequency, defect recording, rework
- WIP levels, FIFO / kanban usage, batch sizes
- Raw material storage and handling
- Safety: PPE, guards, signage, emergency exits
- Training records and operator skill coverage

Production — shift-specific checks
- Handover process at shift change (checklist or verbal, presence of logbook)
- Machine warm-up and cooldown procedures documented and followed
- Preventive maintenance done and recorded per shift
- Shift-level downtime logging and cause recording
- Overtime or fatigue indicators (long hours, short breaks, errors)
- Lighting, noise, and supervision adequacy during night shift

Printers (3-shift operations)
- Confirm printer shift roster and times (TBC) and document
- Check ink/supply changeover procedures and stock at shift handover
- Verify scheduled maintenance frequency accommodates 3-shift operations
- Assess print quality consistency between shifts and rework rates
- Check operator training for printers across all shifts

Production planning / Scheduling
- Is there a master schedule and daily plan?
- BOM accuracy and version control
- Forecast vs. actual production tracking
- Lead times, safety stock, reorder triggers
- Coordination between planning and production floor

Despatch / Purchasing / Receiving
- PO process and approvals
- Receiving checks, QC on inbound goods
- Inventory accuracy (physical vs. system)
- Picking & packing standards, shipping documentation
- Returns process and records

Sales / Retail / Marketing
- Order capture flow (phone/online/in-store) for custom orders
- Lead time communication to customers
- Stock display and product presentation
- Pricing accuracy and POS transaction checks
- Marketing materials & signage consistency

HR / Admin
- Role definitions and documented responsibilities
- Payroll process and records access control
- Training, onboarding, and competency checks
- Absence & timekeeping records

Onsite assessment process
- Start with management interview (10–20 min) to set scope
- Tour the area, observe operations in real time
- Interview 1–2 frontline staff; confirm practices
- Run checklist and capture photos for each finding
- Log each finding in this file or spreadsheet with ID
- For critical safety items, escalate immediately to director

Compiling the report
- For each site: include executive summary, top 5 risks, quick wins, medium/long-term actions
- Attach photos and raw checklist log as appendices
- Prioritise actions by severity and ease-of-implementation
- Provide owners, estimated cost, and target completion dates

File naming and attachments
- Use this pattern for photos: SITE_YYYYMMDD_LOCATION_SHORTDESC.jpg
- Store attachments in a single site folder named `site-SITE-yyyy-mm-dd` in your working folder

Spreadsheet/Log recommendation
- Maintain a simple CSV or Google Sheet with columns matching the Observation entry template for tracking and follow-up.
