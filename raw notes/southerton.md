notes from my visit of the southerton branch (sth) on monday 21 september 2026
for context the southerton branch came after the company in 2022 acquired the machinery of a company that was being closed called City Plastics Industries 
they operated at that exact site and RP saw it fit to acquire the machinery of the entire company
and not move machinery to new premises 
acquistion involved machinery only not intellectual assets
the fleet at that location includes 4 bagmakers, 1 flexo printer(central impression) and a slitter and 4 core extruder
on the day of the visit only two machines were opperational, the printer and one BM
the printer is german make and good quality rivalling gravure printers in output quality
at this factory the man in charge is our Quality Assurance officer and there is a production guy who oversees ops inside the factory 
besides the operators there also exisits a guy from the engineering dept/workshop
there are plans to open a walk in shop there to sell to walk in customers
the place has already been setup and it was schedulled to be open by 1 sept 2026 but it is still not open as of 22/09/2026
this factory has no functioning extruder so they rely solely on WIP made in Msasa HQ 
distance between the two facotries is 12km 30minutes under heavy traffic (according to GMaps)
for rolls that need bagmaking the BM on site will handle that and they will exit the facotry as finished goods with the customer collecting them from there, there is no despatch staff or warehouse so the prod manager on site handles this
then there are some WIP from the printer that need slitting they need to retunr to msasa for slitting as there is no functioning slitter there
it's the QA officer's role to liase with prod planning in msasa to see what jobs are up for the taking in southerton

the printer in sth is massive and needs an MOQ of a 1ton (kg) to make economic sense for prod
but that is not always the case and there are instances of  the printer running small jobs less 500kg
which is costly for setting up and cleaning as claimed by both onsite prod manager and qa officer that the printer needs between 50-100 litres of solvent to prepare for the next job
in terms of documentation this factory uses the exact same type of docs used at HQ
if sth wants rm they fill out the rm req book and msasa releases material and it is taken by any of the pool cars available
hq sales team has only been to sth a couple of times and did not have an appreciation for the facility
no particular reason why though
their eng guy is well versed in electricals not so much mechanical
the sales shop on site will take one person from hq to man the front office

like anywhere in zim loadshedding is an issue but sth power is quite stable
