the extruders do blown film extrusion
each extruder has an operator
the operator mixes material in a large horizontally half cut drum
from  there the material is loaded with smaller buckets into the mixer in the machine
operators run orders according to stated requirements on the works order
they have a per order target weight in kgs 
the film is winded on rolls
which each roll having a target weight to complete
multiple machines can run the same works order
production is recored per roll complete into the extrusion production book
Finished goods will take these books and verify if the operators recorded and calculated the correct balances at the close of their shift
each finished roll is written (by the operator) it's weight and customer name and weighed and given a label but on the stock take I realised that some do not have a label just a simple marker writing with customer name and weight this is usually the case for WIP rolls  
I have attached the finished good label in the repo 
there is also no real time update on production state
this label is used across production stages, not just extrusion
from extrusion the product moves on to next stage
if exstrusion is it's only stop it get label and moved finished goods
if not it moves to next department
