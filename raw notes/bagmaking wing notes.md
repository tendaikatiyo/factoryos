bag making wing houses the bagmaking operations
each bag maker has an operator
they operate on rolls finished from either extrusion or printing depending on the route of the job
bag making wing also houses the core machine and slitting operations
the core machine is used to manufacture paper cores most of them are sold to customers 
but some are retained as stock for use internal ops and some are used for handheld pallet wraps which will sell here
a job can also exit prod via slitting
