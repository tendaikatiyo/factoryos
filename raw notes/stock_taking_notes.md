on the last saturday of every month the company does a stocktake
staff is allocated different tasks on the day
some continue with regular ops
majority of the staff counts finished goods and work in progress goods
there are some who do reconcilliation after stock take is done

during the stock taking staff go around in pairs recording details of the stock 
one record their version and another verifies details if both agree then a stock recoreded card is put on the stock item
completed cards are collected during the process and after for recon by sales and accounts teams
process involves inspecting written marked up labels on product and labels and writing details on the card
